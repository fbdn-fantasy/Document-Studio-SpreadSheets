Document Studio Spreadsheets for 64-bit Intel and AMD Linux

Requires glibc 2.39 or newer, GTK 3, and WebKitGTK 4.1.

Extract the archive. Open a terminal in the extracted folder and run

    ./install.sh

Your package manager will show you what it needs to install. You may be
asked for your administrator password. An internet connection may be
needed to download system libraries.

After installation, open Document Studio Spreadsheets from your application
menu. You can remove the extracted folder and archive. To uninstall the app,
use your distribution's software manager.

The LICENSE file contains the application license.
